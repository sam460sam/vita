# Vyta — TikTok / YouTube Shorts UGC ad (July 2026)

30-second vertical (9:16, 1080×1920) UGC-style ad for **Vyta: Life OS**
([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/it/app/vyta-life-os/id6776238780)),
produced with Higgsfield (Soul 2.0 creator + Seedance 2.0 clips) and real
in-app screenshots captured from this repo (English demo dataset,
`marketing/captures/`).

## Format

One continuous talking-head creator; real app screenshots pop in as overlay
cards (~1.4 s each) while she talks; hook plate on top + word-by-word
captions at the bottom, burned in. English (US) voice — the Seedance
pipeline mangles other languages; an Italian VO variant can be dubbed on
request.

## Script (86 words ≈ 30 s)

**Hook** — `I deleted six apps in one day.`

| Beat | Card (screenshot) | Line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `app_today.png` | So I switched to Vyta — one dashboard for my whole day. |
| 2 | `app_activity.png` | My workouts close three rings, Apple Watch style. |
| 3 | `app_habits.png` | Habits get streaks — I'm on day twenty-one of water. |
| 4 | `app_projects.png` | Projects track themselves — every task bumps that percent. |
| 5 | `app_journal.png` | There's a mood journal — wait, it maps your whole month. |
| 6 | `app_finances.png` | And get this: budgets — it shows where every euro goes. |
| 7 | `app_goals.png` | Goals link to habits, so progress updates itself. |

**Closer** — `And everything stays on your phone — nothing leaves it. Get
Vyta on the App Store.` (creator picks up her phone, screen turned away)

## Deliverable

- `final_captioned.mp4` — hosted URL in the session notes; ready to upload
  as-is to TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

## Suggested captions

- TikTok: *I replaced six apps with one — Vyta: Life OS 📱 #lifeos
  #productivity #habittracker #selfimprovement*
- YouTube Shorts: *Vyta: Life OS — activity rings, habits, projects, mood
  journal, budgets. All offline, all in one app.*
