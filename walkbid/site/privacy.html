Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

Data Not Collected

The short version

WalkBid keeps everything on your device. We — the developer — do not collect, store, see, sell, or share any of your data. There is no account and no login. Deleting the app deletes your data.

What WalkBid stores (only on your device)

Your clients, jobs, estimates, contracts, signatures, photos, generated PDFs, daily logs and payment records are stored locally on your iPhone (in on-device storage and the app’s files). This information never leaves your device unless you choose to share it.

Sharing is something you do, not us

When you tap Share — to send a proposal, a signed PDF, a payment request, or a backup — iOS hands that file or message to the recipient and app you pick (Messages, Mail, etc.). That is your action with your chosen recipient. WalkBid operates no server and receives nothing.

Optional “Live” AI mode (off by default)

WalkBid’s AI assistance runs on-device by default and makes no network requests. You may optionally switch to a Live mode and configure your own proxy URL. Only then, and only for that request, the text you dictate and your price book are sent to your own endpoint and on to Anthropic to generate a draft. This is opt-in, controlled by you, and the WalkBid developer still receives nothing.

No tracking, no ads, no analytics

WalkBid contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party trackers. We do not use the advertising identifier and do not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt because we track nothing.

Children

WalkBid is a business tool intended for contractors and is not directed at children.

Your control & deletion

You control all data on your device: clear it in Settings, export or import a backup, or delete the app to remove everything.
