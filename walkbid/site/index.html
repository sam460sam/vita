Built for the owner-operator

A tool that lives next to a plate compactor — not a SaaS dashboard.

1

Estimate in 90 seconds

Build from your own price book with on-site square-foot and cubic-yard calculators. Share a branded proposal PDF by text or email.

2

Sign on the spot

One tap turns an estimate into a contract. The client signs on your phone — stamped with the time, the place, and a SHA-256 fingerprint you can verify.

3

Change orders, signed

Photograph what changed, price it, hand over the phone, get a signature. Every signed change order adds a payment automatically.

4

Protect your margin

Deposit, progress and final on a glanceable bar. “Deposit not collected — don’t break ground.” Request payment with your Zelle/check/ACH in a tap.

5

Proof in one tap

Contract, signed change orders, geotagged photo timeline, daily logs, payment ledger and every fingerprint — one PDF for disputes or small-claims.

6

Voice & Spanish

Dictate an estimate or change order. Log the day in English or Spanish. Everything works offline, in the sun, with gloves on.
