## Description

**Every change order signed. Every job paid.**

WalkBid is the field app for US hardscape, concrete and paving contractors. It
turns verbal “yeah, go ahead” into signed, time-stamped, photo-backed
documents — so the extra work you do is the extra work you get paid for.

Built for one person: the owner-operator, in gloves, in the sun, on a site with
no signal. Everything works offline.

WHAT IT DOES
• Estimates in under 90 seconds — build from your own price book with on-site
  square-foot and cubic-yard calculators. Share a branded proposal PDF by text
  or email before you leave the driveway.
• Contracts you can sign on the spot — one tap turns an estimate into a
  contract. The client signs on your phone; WalkBid stamps it with the time,
  the place, and a SHA-256 fingerprint you can verify later.
• Change orders signed where the work happens — photograph what changed, price
  it, hand over the phone, get a signature. Every signed change order adds a
  payment automatically.
• Payment protection — deposit, progress and final milestones on a glanceable
  bar. “Deposit not collected — don’t break ground” keeps you honest. Request
  payment by text with your Zelle, check or ACH details in one tap.
• A proof package in one tap — contract, signed change orders, geotagged photo
  timeline, daily logs, payment ledger and every fingerprint in a single PDF
  for disputes or small-claims.
• Voice and Spanish — dictate an estimate or change order; log the day in
  English or Spanish.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
WalkBid stores everything locally. Nothing is collected or uploaded. Back up to
a single file whenever you want.

WalkBid is not a cost database and it isn’t legal advice. It’s the tool that
makes sure the work you did on a handshake doesn’t cost you the job.
